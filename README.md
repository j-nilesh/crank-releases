# Crank

A reading browser that works out what each part of a page is for, and removes
what you've told it you don't want. You can always see what it changed, and
put any page back exactly as published.

## Download

**[Download Crank for Mac](https://github.com/j-nilesh/crank-releases/releases/latest/download/Crank-arm64.dmg)**

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 13 Ventura or later.

## Install

1. Open the downloaded `.dmg`.
2. Drag **Crank** into **Applications**. Crank can only keep itself up to date
   from Applications.
3. Open Crank from Applications. macOS asks whether to open an app downloaded
   from the internet — choose **Open**. Crank is signed and notarized by Apple.

## Updates

Crank checks for a new version when it starts and every six hours. When one is
ready you'll see **Restart to update**. If you miss it, the update installs the
next time you quit and reopen Crank.

## Privacy

That update check is the only network request Crank makes on its own.
Everything Crank records about the pages you read stays on your Mac and is never
sent anywhere. Sites like your bank are excluded entirely; you can change that
list, and how much is recorded, in **Crank ▸ Settings…**.

To send a report, **File ▸ Export Diagnostics…** makes a file you choose where to
save and whether to share.
